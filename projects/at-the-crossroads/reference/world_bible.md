# World & Style Bible: "At the Crossroads" (Take 2 / Meta AI Edition)

This document establishes the artistic rules, geographic environments, color palettes, and panel framing conventions across the complete 10-Track arc of *"At the Crossroads"*.

---

## 1. Master Style Anchor (Append to All Meta AI Prompts)

Every single panel prompt must include this core visual signature:
```text
Style: Highly detailed modern graphic novel panel, crisp clean ink linework with fine cross-hatching, rich digital texture, muted but high-contrast cinematic lighting, clean cel and gradient shading, no messy watercolor wash, no blur.
```

### Why this style?
- **Ink Precision:** Provides crisp edges for comic printing at 300 DPI.
- **Controlled Texture:** Evokes gritty southern gothic folklore without becoming a chaotic watercolor mess.
- **Dynamic Shading:** Gives dramatic depth to faces, instruments, and atmospheric gloom.
- **Meta AI Consistency:** By avoiding buzzwords like "photorealistic" or "hyper-detailed", Meta AI generates high-cohesion comic art panels.

## 2. Setting & Geographic Progression

The story follows a deliberate geographic and atmospheric journey across 10 Tracks:

### Tracks 1–3: The Arid Western Dustbowl (Late 1970s – Early 1980s)
* **Visual Key:** Dilapidated wooden farmhouses, cracked dirt fields, rusted pickup trucks, blinding afternoon heat, barbed wire fences, and roadhouse dive bars behind chicken wire.
* **Palette:** Desaturated earth tones, bleached ochre, dusty faded denim blue, rusted orange, and hazy amber dawn/sunset.
* **Lighting:** Harsh direct sunlight with hard-edged shadows, transitioning to lonely headlights cutting through desert dust.

### Tracks 4–7: The Mississippi Delta & The Rise (The Crossroads to Stardom)
* **Visual Key:** Humid bayous, moss-draped cypress trees, murky waters, dirt crossroads at midnight, rain-slicked southern highways, transitioning into smoke-filled Nashville boardrooms and roaring stadium concert stages.
* **Palette:** Deep bayou greens, muddy umbers, murky river blues, punctuated by the dazzling white glare of spotlights and gold accents of wealth.
* **Lighting:** Chiaroscuro high contrast, eerie sodium streetlamp amber, neon tavern signs (beer red & cobalt blue), blinding stage pyrotechnics.

### Tracks 8–10: The Fall, Penance & The Reckoning
* **Visual Key:** Weathered wooden country chapels, rain-lashed country cemeteries, desolate Highway 61 crossroads, roaring iron motorcycles with burning headlights, and the fiery apocalyptic threshold between earth and hell.
* **Palette:** Charcoal soot, deep midnight indigo, blood crimson, sulfur yellow, and cold ash grey.
* **Lighting:** Flashing lightning, incandescent orange-red hellfire, and stark silhouette chiaroscuro.

## 3. Key Canonical Locations

### Location 1: The Dilapidated Farmhouse (Track 1)
* **Visual Anchor:**
  ```text
  A dilapidated wooden farmhouse surrounded by dry, cracked dirt fields in the late 1970s rural West, peeling paint, rusted farm machinery, sagging front porch, vast lonely horizon under pale bleached sky.
  ```

### Location 2: The Crossroads at Midnight (Tracks 2 & 5)
* **Visual Anchor:**
  ```text
  A deserted rural dirt crossroads intersection at midnight in the Mississippi Delta, surrounded by withered cornfields and twisted dead trees, solitary flickering yellow sodium vapor streetlamp casting long eerie shadows, low swirling ground fog, ominous dark sky.
  ```

### Location 3: The Honky-Tonk Dive Bar (Tracks 2 & 4)
* **Visual Anchor:**
  ```text
  Interior of a dingy, smoky roadside tavern, peeling wood-paneled walls lined with faded concert posters, glowing neon beer signs casting blue and red light through thick cigarette haze, battered wooden bar counter, vintage neon jukebox glowing in the corner.
  ```

### Location 4: The Country Chapel (Track 8)
* **Visual Anchor:**
  ```text
  A modest weathered white wooden country church with a modest steeple in rural Mississippi, surrounded by quiet weeping willow trees and cracked dirt path, bathed in solemn late-afternoon amber light.
  ```

### Location 5: The Midnight Highway Confrontation (Tracks 9 & 10)
* **Visual Anchor:**
  ```text
  A deserted two-lane blacktop highway cutting through flat Mississippi Delta swamps at midnight, asphalt wet with recent rain reflecting headlights, wall of cypress trees dripping Spanish moss, roaring choppers surrounding a solitary figure in the road.
  ```

## 4. Lettering & Caption Rule

For panels that include comic voiceover / story narration:
```text
Include a torn paper caption box at the top with the exact text: "[CAPTION TEXT]". Use a messy handwritten marker font.
```
* **Rule:** Keep caption text verbatim inside quotation marks.
* **Alternative:** For letterers assembling on separate layers in Krita, Photoshop, or Clip Studio Paint, the caption sentence can be removed from the prompt to yield clean line art.
