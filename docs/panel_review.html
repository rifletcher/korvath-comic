Chapter 1: The Slumbering Serpent of Sarkesh — Director Review Deck
⚔️ PANEL 1: THE COLD DAWN WINDS CANONICAL ANCHOR MASTERPIECE

Script Action: The barbarian warrior stands solitary upon a rocky mountain precipice overlooking mist-shrouded ruins and an ancient cliff fortress, heavy wolf-fur mantle draped over broad scarred bronze shoulders, leather harness crossing chest, straight steel broadsword in hand.

Captions: "THE COLD DAWN WINDS HOWL THROUGH THE RUINS... HE WALKS ALONE -- SWORD IN HAND. NO MASTER. NO GOD. ONLY THE BLADE." / "THE BARBARIAN. LONE SWORD. LONE SURVIVOR."

⚔️ PANEL 2: EYES IN THE DUST DIRECTOR APPROVED

Script Action: Korvath kneels upon one knee in the wind-whipped mountain sand and gravel ruins, inspecting freshly disturbed footprints near an ancient carved stone skull, scarred weathered fingers resting securely on the wire-bound hilt of his massive broadsword.

Caption: "He smelled the bitter smoke of grey lotus on the mountain wind—and the cold oil of drawn blades."

⚔️ PANEL 3: THE CRYPT OF THE SERPENT NEW DIRECTOR RENDER

Script Action: Korvath heaves aside an ancient shattered granite door slab carved with writhing serpent reliefs, descending down steep stone stairs into the shadowed crypt, holding high a blazing pine pitch torch.

Caption: "Into the bowels of forgotten Sarkesh he strode, where gods older than mankind slept in stagnant gloom."
